Largest maths block; never below 13 marks. Bayes' theorem has appeared every year.

## Official syllabus

> Counting (permutations and combinations), probability axioms, sample space, events, independent events, mutually exclusive events, marginal, conditional and joint probability, Bayes theorem, conditional expectation and variance, mean, median, mode and standard deviation, correlation and covariance, random variables, discrete random variables and probability mass functions, uniform, Bernoulli, binomial distribution, continuous random variables and probability distribution function, uniform, exponential, Poisson, normal, standard normal, t-distribution, chi-squared distributions, cumulative distribution function, conditional PDF, central limit theorem, confidence interval, z-test, t-test, chi-squared test.

## Topic plan

Topic pages will be written in this order, following the [notes playbook](https://github.com/ashyou09/Gate-da/blob/main/NOTES_PLAYBOOK.md). Each one gets a topic map, teaching from basics, a summary, solved PYQs and predicted 2027 questions.

1. Counting & probability axioms <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
2. Conditional probability, independence & Bayes' theorem <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
3. Random variables, expectation, variance, covariance <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
4. Discrete distributions: uniform, Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
5. Continuous distributions: uniform, exponential, normal <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
6. CDF/PDF manipulation & transformations <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
7. Conditional expectation & law of total variance <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
8. Descriptive statistics & correlation <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
9. CLT & confidence intervals <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
10. Hypothesis tests: z, t, chi-squared <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
11. t and chi-squared distributions <span class="tag low">○ LOW</span>
