## How every topic page is built

Each page follows the same order, so you always know where to look:

1. **Topic map**: a mind map of every sub-topic on the page.
2. **Teach**: concepts from basics, with intuition first and then formulas in boxes. Explained the way a teacher would, not as a list of bullets.
3. **Summary**: the important points and traps, to re-read the night before the exam.
4. **Test yourself (A)**: real GATE DA PYQs (2024–2026), solved step by step and matched to the official key.
5. **Test yourself (B)**: predicted 2027-style questions (MCQ / MSQ / NAT) with full solutions.
