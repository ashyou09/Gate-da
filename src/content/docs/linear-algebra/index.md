## Official syllabus

> Vector space, subspaces, linear dependence and independence of vectors, matrices, projection matrix, orthogonal matrix, idempotent matrix, partition matrix and their properties, quadratic forms, systems of linear equations and solutions; Gaussian elimination, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, determinant, rank, nullity, projections, LU decomposition, singular value decomposition.

## Topic plan

Topic pages will be written in this order, following the [notes playbook](https://github.com/ashyou09/Gate-da/blob/main/NOTES_PLAYBOOK.md). Each one gets a topic map, teaching from basics, a summary, solved PYQs and predicted 2027 questions.

1. Vector spaces, subspaces, basis, independence <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
2. Rank, nullity & rank-nullity theorem <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
3. Systems of linear equations & Gaussian elimination <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
4. Determinants & special matrices (orthogonal, idempotent, projection) <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
5. Eigenvalues & eigenvectors <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
6. Quadratic forms & definiteness <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
7. LU decomposition <span class="tag low">○ LOW</span>
8. SVD & its link to PCA <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
