## Official syllabus

> Search: informed, uninformed, adversarial; logic: propositional, predicate; reasoning under uncertainty: conditional independence representation, exact inference through variable elimination, and approximate inference through sampling.

## Topic plan

Topic pages will be written in this order, following the [notes playbook](https://github.com/ashyou09/Gate-da/blob/main/NOTES_PLAYBOOK.md). Each one gets a topic map, teaching from basics, a summary, solved PYQs and predicted 2027 questions.

1. Uninformed search: BFS, DFS, UCS, IDDFS <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
2. Informed search: greedy best-first, A*, admissibility & consistency <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
3. Adversarial search: minimax & alpha-beta pruning <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
4. Propositional logic: equivalences, CNF, resolution <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
5. Predicate (first-order) logic <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
6. Bayesian networks & conditional independence <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
7. Exact inference: variable elimination <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
8. Approximate inference: sampling <span class="tag low">○ LOW</span>
