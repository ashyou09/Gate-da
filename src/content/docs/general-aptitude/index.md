## Official syllabus

> Verbal Aptitude (grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension, verbal analogies), Quantitative Aptitude (data interpretation, ratios, percentages, permutations & combinations, geometry, mensuration, series, logarithms), Analytical Aptitude (logic, deduction, induction, analogy, number relations) and Spatial Aptitude (transformation of shapes, paper folding, cutting, patterns).

## Topic plan

Topic pages will be written in this order, following the [notes playbook](https://github.com/ashyou09/Gate-da/blob/main/NOTES_PLAYBOOK.md). Each one gets a topic map, teaching from basics, a summary, solved PYQs and predicted 2027 questions.

1. Verbal: grammar & vocabulary <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
2. Verbal: reading comprehension & critical reasoning <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
3. Quant: percentages, ratios, averages, mixtures <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
4. Quant: time-speed-work, ages, profit-loss <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
5. Quant: counting & probability basics <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
6. Quant: data interpretation (tables, charts) <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
7. Analytical: logic, syllogisms, arrangements <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
8. Spatial: folding, cutting, rotation, patterns <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
