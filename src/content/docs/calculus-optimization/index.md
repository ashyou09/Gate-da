## Official syllabus

> Functions of a single variable, limit, continuity and differentiability, Taylor series, maxima and minima, optimization involving a single variable.

## Topic plan

Topic pages will be written in this order, following the [notes playbook](https://github.com/ashyou09/Gate-da/blob/main/NOTES_PLAYBOOK.md). Each one gets a topic map, teaching from basics, a summary, solved PYQs and predicted 2027 questions.

1. Limits, continuity & differentiability <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
2. Taylor series <span class="tag med">◆ MED</span>
3. Maxima & minima (single and multivariable) <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
4. Convexity <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
5. Gradient descent as an update rule <span class="tag high">★ HIGH</span>
6. Constrained optimization & Lagrange multipliers <span class="tag low">○ LOW</span>
