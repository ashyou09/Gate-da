## Official syllabus

> Verbal Aptitude (grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension, verbal analogies), Quantitative Aptitude (data interpretation, ratios, percentages, permutations & combinations, geometry, mensuration, series, logarithms), Analytical Aptitude (logic, deduction, induction, analogy, number relations) and Spatial Aptitude (transformation of shapes, paper folding, cutting, patterns).
